About the district

Vlohy Warsaw apartment rental is a unique offer for those who regularly fly or travel by train in Poland. Włochy borders Chopin Airport directly: from the apartment to the terminal - 3-5 minutes by car or SKM.

Warszawa Zachodnia (Warsaw Central Railway Junction) is also within the precinct or within walking distance. Connections to all Polish cities are 5 minutes' walk away. This makes Włochy a unique choice for those who live 'between cities'.

New condominiums 2018-2024 offer modern decoration at a lower price than in Wola or Mokotówie. Aircraft noise is an unavoidable trade-off.

Transport and location

Włochy is west-south of Warsaw, right next to Chopin Airport. Centrum is 8–12 km away.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent in Włochy Warsaw?

Włochy 2026: 68–90 PLN/m²/month. Two-room apartment: 3,500–5,000 PLN. A relatively quieter market with no peak seasonal demand.

Can you hear planes in Włochy apartments?

Yes, in the area of ​​the runway, the noise is significant, especially at night. We recommend checking noise zones on maps.google.pl and choosing apartments with soundproof windows.
