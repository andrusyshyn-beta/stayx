About the district

Renting Wilanów Warsaw is the choice of the diplomatic corps, top managers of multinational corporations and wealthy families who value elite buildings and silence. Wilanów is associated with the Wilanów Palace (Polish Versailles), diplomatic residences and premium residential complexes.

The strong point is new high-quality residential complexes with underground parking, concierge and security: Miasteczko Wilanów, Nowe Wilanów, luxurious villas with gardens. Weak - the complete absence of the subway. It takes 30-45 minutes to get to the center by bus E-1, depending on the traffic on Puławska.

The price range is the widest in Warsaw: from PLN 6,000 for a modest two-room apartment to PLN 14,000+ for a villa with a garden or a penthouse. The luxury segment here is the deepest among all districts of Warsaw.

Transport and location

Wilanów is the extreme south of Warsaw, bordering Ursynów and Konstancin-Jeziorna. Centrum is 12–15 km away. Without a car or high-quality bus service, the site is isolated.

  • Buses: E-1 (high-speed to Centrum), 519, 709
  • Auto: S79 / Puławska — main arteries
  • Traffic jams: Puławska at peak — 40–60 min
  • Without a subway is a fundamental limitation

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent in Vilianov 2026?

Rent in Wilanów — PLN 95-130/m²/month. Two-bedroom: PLN 6,000-10,000, three-bedroom villa: PLN 9,000-14,000+. Wilanów is a premium segment with the widest price range among the districts of Warsaw.

Is there a subway in Wilanów?

No. Wilanów does not have a subway. The main means of transport are buses (E-1 high-speed) and cars. Time to the center: 30-45 minutes without traffic, 60+ minutes per hour. peak.

Is Wilanów good for families?

Yes, this is one of the best areas for families with children: international schools, new developments with playgrounds, safety. But you need a budget of PLN 7,000-8,000/month for 2-3 rooms.

Wilanów or Ursynów — which is better?

Wilanów is premium, more expensive, no metro, new development. Ursynów — middle class, M1, more affordable, better for working in the center. For families without a car — Ursynów wins. For the wealthy with a car — Wilanów.
