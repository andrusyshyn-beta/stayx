01 · Legal Status

Legal status of Ukrainians in Poland after March 5, 2026

On March 5, 2026, an updated Special Act for citizens of Ukraine came into force. Poland shifted from an emergency regime to the general temporary protection system, gradually aligning Ukrainian citizens' rights with other foreigners. Most existing statuses are automatically extended. Fundacja PFM, March 2026

⚠️

Important: PESEL UKR is an identification number, not a residence permit. For long-term legal stay, you need a separate basis: a CUKR card, a karta pobytu, or another permit.

Three Legalization Paths in 2026

Active Status

Auto-extension of UKR → until March 4, 2027

Kartas pobytu and PESEL UKR statuses active before March 4, 2026, are automatically extended to March 4, 2027 — without filing any applications. No action needed.

Condition: had active status before March 4, 2026. Source: Warszawa19115, April 2026
New Card 2026

CUKR Card — 3-year residence permit

A new 3-year residence permit with full access to the labor market without separate work permits. Applied online via the MOS system.

Condition: continuous UKR status for 365+ days + active status at application. Source: Fundacja PFM, March 2026
General Order

Karta pobytu (Residence Card)

Temporary residence permit for 1 to 3 years through employment, studies, business, or family reunification. Processing time in Warsaw is 4–12 months.

Term: 4–12 months in Warsaw (slower Mazovian Voivodship Office). Source: howtopoland.com, 2026
💡

App Name Change: Since March 2026, the "mObywatel / Diia.pl" app is renamed to "Dokument ochrony czasowej". The functionality remains the same — confirming temporary protection status at the border and within Poland. how2go.info, June 2026

Changes in Social Payouts

Since February 2026, the 800+ child benefit (świadczenie wychowawcze) is tied to official employment. Without official work or business activity, it is impossible to receive 800+. This represents a transition from a humanitarian to a work-based support model. poland-consult.com, March 2026

Leaving Poland for more than 30 days leads to automatic loss of UKR status (status changes to NUE) and associated rights. If you plan a trip to Ukraine longer than a month, consult a lawyer beforehand. Warszawa19115, April 2026

02 · Entry

Entering Poland from Ukraine: documents at the border

Citizens of Ukraine enter Poland visa-free for up to 90 days in a 180-day period. For longer stays, you need one of the legalization paths described above.

1

Biometric Foreign Passport

Primary and sufficient document for entry. Internal passport (ID card) is not accepted at the border. Validity must exceed the planned stay.

Mandatory
2

Dokument ochrony czasowej (formerly Diia.pl)

If you have active UKR status, the app confirms it at the border. The app was renamed in March 2026; download the updated version before travel.

For active UKR status holders
3

Karta pobytu / CUKR (if issued)

Physical karta pobytu allows staying in Poland and traveling within the Schengen zone for up to 90 days in a 180-day window. You must carry the physical card.

Expands travel rights
4

Confirmation of Stay Purpose and Address

The border guard may ask for the address where you will stay, the purpose of your trip, and a contact in Poland. Having a rental agreement or hotel booking speeds up crossing.

Recommended to carry
🚌

How to get there: Warsaw–Kyiv bus takes 12–18 hours, starting from 30 EUR (FlixBus, LuxExpress). Train via Przemyśl takes 12–14 hours. Car via crossings like Dorohusk or Krościenko. Flight: Warsaw Chopin (WAW) offers direct connections via third countries.

03 · First Steps

First steps in Warsaw: post-arrival action plan

The first week in Warsaw is document-intensive. Here is the correct order of actions to keep things smooth.

01

Temporary housing for the first week

Hotel, hostel, or Airbnb for 5–10 days. Do not rush into long-term rentals; explore the districts first. Budget: 80–200 PLN/night.

02

Registering UKR status or getting PESEL (days 1–2)

Go to Urząd Dzielnicy or gmina. If you recently arrived and do not have UKR status, apply on the basis of temporary protection. If you have it, proceed to meldunek. Info: warszawa19115.pl

Unlocks: NFZ healthcare, banks, state services
03

Meldunek — registration of residence address (days 2–4)

After signing the rental agreement, go immediately to Urząd Dzielnicy. Passport + lease contract + completed meldunek form. Free, takes 15–20 mins. The landlord cannot refuse.

Does not require landlord's formal consent
04

PESEL (days 3–5)

Issued with meldunek or via simplified procedure for temporary protection holders. Handled at Urząd Dzielnicy. Opens bank accounts and NFZ healthcare access.

Time: 1–7 business days
05

Bank Account (days 5–7)

With PESEL: PKO BP, ING, mBank, Santander — 30–60 mins at a branch. Without PESEL: Revolut or Wise — online in 15 mins. Most landlords and employers require a Polish account.

Needed for rent transfers and salary payments
06

Registration with NFZ or private healthcare (weeks 1–2)

Through employer: NFZ is automatic with umowa o pracę. Self-employed: via ZUS. Private: Medicover/LuxMed — 150–300 PLN/month, no queues, English-speaking staff.

Medicover and LuxMed have English support
07

Karta pobytu or CUKR (months 1–3)

If staying longer than 90 days, apply via MOS (mos.gov.pl). A stamp in your passport after submission is proof of legal stay during processing.

Warsaw: 4–12 months wait time
04 · Apartment Rental

Apartment rental in Warsaw: details for Ukrainians

Polish law does not make distinctions based on citizenship when renting. In practice, landlords are more cautious with foreigners, especially those newly arrived without a PESEL or a local network of contacts.

What a Foreigner Needs to Rent

Minimum package: passport + proof of income (umowa o pracę or B2B invoices for 3 months). For Najem Okazjonalny, a backup address (lokal zastępczy) is also required. Full documents list is in "Documents for Renting", and step-by-step instructions are in "How to Rent as a Foreigner".

05 · Work & Business

Work and business in Warsaw for Ukrainians

UKR status holders had the right to work without permits until March 4, 2026. Under the new regime, work rights depend on the residency basis:

🟢

CUKR Card — full access to the labor market

The new 3-year CUKR card provides the right to work without any additional permits — same as EU citizens. The most convenient option for 2026–2027.

🟡

Karta pobytu via employer

The employer registers a work invitation (oświadczenie or zezwolenie na pracę). While processing (4–12 months), a passport stamp allows legal work. The most common path for new arrivals.

🔵

Business: JDG (sole proprietorship)

Single-person business registration via CEIDG is free. Ukrainians can register a JDG with a karta pobytu or a CUKR card. ZUS social tax rates for small business range from 400 to 1,800 PLN/month depending on relief programs.

Average net salary in Warsaw is 8,000–9,000 PLN in IT, and 5,000–7,000 PLN in other fields. The minimum wage in 2026 is 4,666 PLN gross. GUS, Migrantexpert, 2026

06 · Healthcare

Healthcare and insurance in Warsaw

Warsaw offers an excellent medical infrastructure, blending the public NFZ system with highly developed private networks (Medicover, LuxMed, Enel-Med).

NFZ

Narodowy Fundusz Zdrowia — public insurance

Free healthcare for all who pay ZUS contributions or have special status. Under the new rules, access is via employment or self-contributions. Drawback: queues — 2–12 weeks wait for a specialist.

Priv

Private Insurance — Medicover, LuxMed

Subscriptions range from 150 to 300 PLN/month, offering next-day appointments and English-speaking doctors. Highly recommended for the first few months until you obtain public coverage.

Best choice for the first months
OC

OC Najemcy — Tenant Liability Insurance

Covers accidental damage to property (flooding neighbors, breaking valuable items). Costs 100–250 PLN/year. Some landlords require this during contract signing.

🏥

Emergency cases are always free — regardless of insurance status. SOR (Szpitalny Oddział Ratunkowy) handles emergencies without referrals. Emergency numbers: 999 (ambulance), 112 (general).

08 · Children & Education

Schools and kindergartens in Warsaw for Ukrainian children

Poland ensures free school education for all children legally residing in the country, regardless of citizenship. Warsaw schools have extensive experience with Ukrainian students since 2022.

🏫

Public School (rejonizacja)

Every child is entitled to a place at the school corresponding to their registration address (meldunek). Polish language proficiency is not an admission requirement. Most schools provide free Polish language lessons for foreign pupils.

🌍

International and Private Schools

American School of Warsaw, British School, French School — for those seeking English or French education. Highest concentration in Wilanów and Mokotów. Cost: 2,000–5,000+ EUR/year.

🍼

Kindergartens (żłobek & przedszkole)

Public nurseries (żłobek, 0–3 years): waiting list, free or minimal fees. Kindergartens (przedszkole, 3–6 years): subsidized. Private: 1,200–2,500 PLN/month. Enrollment is via the Warszawski System Rekrutacji (WSR) portal in early February.

11 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions about moving to Warsaw

Is it possible to relocate from Ukraine to Warsaw in 2026?+
Yes. Ukrainian citizens can enter Poland visa-free for up to 90 days in a 180-day window. For long-term stays, you must obtain legal residence: a CUKR card (for those who have held continuous UKR status for 365+ days) or a karta pobytu through work, studies, or business. Source: how2go.info, June 2026.
What changes affected the UKR status after March 5, 2026?+
On March 5, 2026, an updated Special Act took effect. PESEL UKR and kartas pobytu active before March 4, 2026, are automatically extended to March 4, 2027. Poland has transitioned to the standard EU temporary protection model. Leaving Poland for more than 30 days results in losing the UKR status. Source: Fundacja PFM, March 2026.
What is the CUKR card and who is eligible?+
The CUKR card is a new 3-year residence permit in Poland for Ukrainian citizens, granting full labor market access. Eligibility requires a continuous UKR status of at least 365 days and holding active UKR status at application. Applications are submitted electronically via MOS (mos.gov.pl). Source: Fundacja PFM, March 2026.
How much money is needed to relocate to Warsaw?+
Initial upfront cost: 1st month rent (4,000–6,000 PLN) + 2-month deposit (8,000–12,000 PLN) + notary and translator (800–1,300 PLN) + temporary housing for 1-2 weeks (800–2,000 PLN) = 14,000–22,000 PLN in total. Monthly expenses run around 5,500–9,500 PLN (rent + utilities + food + transport + health insurance).
How to find work in Warsaw quickly?+
IT professionals: LinkedIn, Pracuj.pl, NoFluffJobs (takes 2–6 weeks). Obtaining a karta pobytu through work requires an oświadczenie from the company. Note: since March 5, 2026, only holders of a CUKR card or karta pobytu are entitled to work without separate permits.
