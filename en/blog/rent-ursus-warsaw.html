About the district

Ursus Warsaw rental is a former factory town (the famous 'Ursus' tractor) that has been transformed into a residential area with new neighborhoods. Attached to Warsaw in 1977, Ursus still retains a certain 'suburban' character.

The KM/SKM railway connects to the center (Warszawa Zachodnia) in 20-25 minutes - better than the bus. The planned westward extension of the M2 could significantly increase prices in the coming years, but the timing has not yet been confirmed.

New condominiums 2018–2024 offer a modern standard at a price that has not yet caught up to Bemowo or Woli. For those who are looking for new apartments within the city for a minimum price, this is the best option.

Transport and location

Ursus is the extreme west of Warsaw, bordered by Włochy (east), Bemowo (north) and Pruszków outside the city limits.

  • KM/SKM Ursus — to Warszawa Zachodnia in 20 min
  • Buses: 147, 156 — to Bemowo and Woli
  • S8 is a high-speed track in Łódź
  • M2 is planned - the terms are not confirmed

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent Ursus 2026?

Ursus 2026: PLN 63–85/m²/month. New two-room apartment: 3,200-4,500 PLN - one of the lowest prices for new apartments in Warsaw.

How to get from Ursus to the center of Warsaw?

KM/SKM: 20-25 minutes to Warszawa Zachodnia. Bus — 30–40 minutes. By car along S8 — 15–20 minutes without traffic jams.

Ursus or Białołęka?

Both are the cheapest new apartments in Warsaw. Ursus is closer to the center (west), there is a railway. Białołęka is the north, only buses, but nature is better.
