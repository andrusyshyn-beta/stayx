About the district

Wawer Warsaw rental is a unique option for a metropolis: forest, river and silence within the administrative borders of Warsaw. Wawer is the most wooded area of ​​the city: pine forests, the Vistula floodplain and walking paths are not a suburb, they are literally in the city.

Private houses and small apartments predominate here. Apartments for rent are relatively limited in choice, but the price is the lowest among all districts of Warsaw. KM/SKM delivers to Centrum in 25-35 minutes.

For whom: families who are ready to sacrifice accessibility for the sake of nature and silence, summer residents from Warsaw and those who seek a "home" style of living without leaving the city.

Transport and location

Wawer is the extreme east and south of Warsaw, the right bank of the Vistula. The centrum is 15–20 km away.

  • KM/SKM Wawer, Międzylesie - to Centrum in 25-35 min
  • Buses: 115, 138 — irregular
  • By car: Route 801, S8 — to the center 20–30 min
  • Bicycle — forests and embankments

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent at Wawer 2026?

Wawer 2026: PLN 62-82/m²/month. Two-room apartment: 3,000–4,200 PLN. The lowest price among all districts of Warsaw according to Investropa.

Is there a subway on Wawer?

No. KM/SKM is the main transport to the center (25-35 minutes). A car significantly improves the quality of living.

Wawer or Białołęka — which is cheaper and greener?

Both are cheap and green. Wawer is the right bank, more forest, more houses, less apartments. Białołęka — the left bank, more new housing complexes and apartments for rent.
