About the district

Byalolenko Warsaw rent is the choice of those who want a new apartment in Warsaw for a minimum price and are ready to sacrifice transport accessibility. Białołęka is the largest market of new buildings in Warsaw: thousands of apartments in new housing complexes with playgrounds, underground parking and modern decoration.

The main disadvantage is the distance. Without a car, Białołęka is practically cut off: bus service takes 45-60 minutes to the center. For families with a car, where both partners drive, this is an acceptable compromise.

The price position is the lowest among areas with full-fledged residential quarters. A two-room new apartment for PLN 3,000-4,200 is a rarity in Warsaw.

Transport and location

Białołęka is the far north of Warsaw, across the Wisła River (right bank). Centrum is 18–22 km away. A car is practically mandatory.

  • Buses: 132, 181, 147 — 45–60 minutes to the center
  • SKM Białołęka — railway station (irregular)
  • By car: S8, droga ekspresowa to the center — 25 min
  • There is no subway, no tram

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent in Białołęka in 2026?

Białołęka 2026: PLN 65–88/m²/month. New two-room apartment: PLN 3,000-4,200 - the lowest price among the new stock in Warsaw.

Is it possible to live in Białołęka without a car?

Technically yes, but very inconvenient: buses to the center take 45-60 minutes and are overcrowded. For daily trips to the center, a car or motorcycle significantly improves the quality of life.

Is Białołęka good for families?

Yes, if you have a car: new residential complex with playgrounds, new schools, green, quiet. But logistics without a car is problematic.
