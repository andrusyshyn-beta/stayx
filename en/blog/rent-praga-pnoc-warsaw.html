About the district

Prague North Warsaw rental is Warsaw's Kreuzberg: a district that transforms before your eyes. Industrial buildings become loft apartments and art spaces, old tenements are renovated, independent cafes and galleries fill the first floors. Praga Północ is the most dynamic district of the city in terms of cultural growth.

M2 (Dworzec Wileński, Szwedzka) provides an excellent connection to the center and Wolą in 10-15 minutes. The rental price is 20-30% lower than in Śródmieście at the same distance from the center. But the neighborhood is uneven: next to an elegant loft can be a house with problems.

A typical tenant in 2026: a young professional in the creative industry, a designer, a journalist or an IT specialist who values ​​atmosphere and price, but does not need family comfort.

Transport and location

Praga Północ is the right bank of the Vistula, opposite Śródmieście. Through Gdańsk Bridge or M2 — 10 minutes to the center.

  • M2: Dworzec Wileński and Szwedzka
  • Trams: 4, 23 — across the bridges to the center
  • Dworzec Wileński is a railway station
  • Bicycle route along the Vistuli

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent at Praga Północ?

Praga Północ 2026: PLN 75-105/m²/month. Studio: PLN 2,600-3,800, two-room: PLN 3,500-5,500. Lofts and new apartments are 10-20% more expensive than the market of the district.

Is it safe to live in Praga Północ?

Security has improved significantly since the 2010s. Szmulowizna and Stara Praga in 2026 are quite comfortable. We recommend checking a specific street and house.

Praga Północ or Praga Południe?

Praga Północ is smaller, more bohemian, M2 in the center of the district, lower price. Praga Południe is bigger, more family friendly, Saska Kępa like a pearl. For young people - Północ. For families - Południe.
