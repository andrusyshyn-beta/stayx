About the district

Mokotów is the largest district in Warsaw by the number of inhabitants and one of the most diverse. Górny Mokotów (Upper) is villas, embassies and quiet streets with chestnut alleys, Dolny Mokotów (Lower) is the modern Domaniewska office cluster, home to IKEA, Samsung, Google. Between them is Pole Mokotowskie, a favorite park of Warsaw residents.

From the point of view of renting an apartment, Mokotów Warsaw is one of the most competitive markets: demand consistently exceeds supply, especially in the segment of 2-3-room apartments for families. The average rental rate is PLN 82/m²/month, which is higher than the Warsaw average (source: Domiporta, 547 ads, Q1 2026).

The district attracts families (schools with rejonizacją, private international), IT specialists (offices in Dolny), and expats, for whom the balance of greenery and accessibility of the center is important.

Transport and location

Mokotuv is located south of the center. Rondo Daszyńskiego - 15 minutes by metro, Dworzec Centralny - 20 minutes. It borders Śródmieście to the north, Wilanów to the east and Ursynów to the south.

  • M1: 5 stations through (Wilanowska → Służew)
  • Trams: 17, 33 — Nowy Świat, Śródmieście
  • Buses: 150+ routes
  • Cycle paths along Puławska and Doliny Służewiecka

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Mokotov in 2026?

The average rental price in Mokotów is PLN 82/m²/month (Domiporta, 547 ads, Q1 2026). Studio: PLN 3,200-4,800, one-room: PLN 3,800-5,800, two-room: PLN 4,800-7,000. Upper Mokotów (Górny) is 10–15% more expensive than Lower.

Is there a subway in Mokotov?

Yes, M1 passes through the whole of Mokotów with 5 stations: Wilanowska, Wierzbno, Racławicka, Służew and Ursynów. Centrum is 15-20 minutes away.

Which district of Mokotov is best for families?

Górny Mokotów (Xaveryów, Sadyba, Sluzewec) is the best for families: more greenery, quieter, schools within walking distance. Dolny Mokotów is better for IT professionals and young people.

How long does it take to find an apartment in Mokotov?

A good apartment in the budget of PLN 5,000-7,000 in Mokotów "costs" 7-14 days. The precinct is in short supply - you should have a package of documents ready and be ready to sign within 24-48 hours.
