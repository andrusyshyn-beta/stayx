About the district

Bemovo Warsaw for rent is one of the most transformed districts in 2026. The extension of the M2 line to the west (new stations Łazurowa, Chrzanów, Karolin) has opened Bemowo to those looking for a new apartment with a metro connection at a lower price than in Wola or Mokotówie.

The area is being actively developed: new residential complexes 2022–2026 offer a standard of underground parking, balconies and modern decoration. Prices per m² are growing faster than in most areas — which makes renting here an attractive investment for owners.

For tenants, this is a chance to get a new apartment near the subway at a price that has not yet "compared" with more central areas.

Transport and location

Bemowo is the west of Warsaw, bordering Wolą in the east and Ursusem in the west. M2 (new stations 2026) passes through the center of the precinct.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent on Bemowo in 2026?

Bemowo 2026: 72–98 PLN/m²/month. Studio: PLN 2,400-3,400, two-room: PLN 3,800-5,200. With the opening of M2 in 2026, prices are rising - we recommend fixing the rate for a longer period.

When did the subway on Bemowo open?

New M2 stations: Łazurowa, Chrzanów and Karolin opened in 2026, significantly increasing the transport accessibility of the precinct and pushing up rental prices.

Bemowo or Wola?

Wola is more central, Google/Samsung offices within walking distance, price is higher. Bemowo is a new residential complex, quieter family character, 10-15% more affordable. With the new M2, the difference in transport has been reduced to a minimum.
