About the district

Targuvek Warsaw rent is one of the most affordable areas of the right bank. The area is traditionally seen as 'working class': prefab houses, old stock, but steady demand from those looking for a cheap apartment near the M2 (Praga Północ borders).

The expected extension of the M2 (Zacisze and Kondratowicza stations) will give Targówek a significant transport advantage and push prices up - as happened with Bemowo after the opening of the new stations in 2026.

For renters with a limited budget of up to PLN 3,500 for a two-room apartment, Targówek is one of the few options in the heart of the city.

Transport and location

Targówek is the north of the right bank, bordering Praga Północ (south) and Białołęka (north). Trams and buses to the center - 20-30 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much is the rent in Targówek in 2026?

Targówek 2026: 63–85 PLN/m²/month. Two-room apartment: 3,200–4,500 PLN. One of the most accessible districts of Warsaw with regular tram service.

Is there a subway at Targówek?

Not yet. M2 is planned to be extended to Zacisze and Kondratowicza stations, but specific dates have not been confirmed. Trams connect the area with M2 (Praga Północ) in 10-15 minutes.

Is Targówek safe?

Generally safe, especially Targówek Mieszkaniowy and Bródno. We recommend checking the specific street.
