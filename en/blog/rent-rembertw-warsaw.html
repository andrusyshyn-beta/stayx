About the district

Rembertów Warsaw for rent is one of the smallest and least known districts of Warsaw. It is dominated by private development, there are several blocks of blocks and very few apartments for long-term rent. But those that are available are the cheapest in Warsaw.

The KM railway provides a connection with the center in 30-40 minutes. A car is practically mandatory for a comfortable stay. The area retains its semi-Soviet 'town' character with local shops and quiet streets.

For whom: those who have a very limited budget and are looking for the cheapest apartment in Warsaw, or those who need silence and proximity to Michuryn Forest.

Transport and location

Rembertów is the far east of Warsaw, bordering Targówek (west) and Wesoła (east). The center is 20 km away.

  • KM Rembertów — to Centrum 30–40 min
  • Buses: 146 — to Targówek
  • By car: Route 637, S17 — to the center 20–25 min
  • Bicycle — forest and natural routes

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent at Rembertów 2026?

Rembertów 2026: 58–78 PLN/m²/month. Two-room apartment: 2,800–3,800 PLN. The lowest price in Warsaw, but also the smallest selection.

Are there shops and cafes on Rembertów?

There is a basic infrastructure: supermarkets, a pharmacy, a market. For restaurants and more choice - Targówek or Praga (20-30 min).

Rembertów or Wesoła?

Both are small and budget. Rembertów is a little closer to Pragi. Wesoła is even smaller and quieter. Both are suitable only for those who are ready for complete detachment from the city rhythm.
