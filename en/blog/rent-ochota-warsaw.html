About the district

Okhota Warsaw rent an apartment — the golden middle class of the Polish rental market. The area between Śródmieście and Mokotów has traditionally attracted academia (University of Warsaw, Politechnika), family renters and those looking for a quiet, central option without paying extra for the address.

Despite the lack of a metro, transport accessibility is excellent: trams and buses take 10-15 minutes to Centrum. The rental price is pleasantly lower than Mokotów with a similar location and quality of life.

Ochota - academic and family character, green courtyards, small parks and special silence, which is a stone's throw from Aleje Jerozolimskie.

Transport and location

Ochota is between Śródmieście (north), Mokotów (south) and Wolą (east). From Aleje Jerozolimskie 2 — 15 minutes on foot to Centrum Warszawy.

  • Trams: 7, 9, 15 - directly to Centrum
  • Buses: 106, 128, 171 — to Mokotów and Woli
  • Varsovie Zachodnia is a railway station near
  • Bike lanes along Aleje Jerozolimskie

Who is Ochota for?

  • Academic environment — teachers, graduate students
  • Families appreciating a central location
  • Those who want between Śródmieście and Mokotów at a lower price
  • Young couples with their first apartment

Pros

  • Central location without central allowance
  • Silence, green yards, academic atmosphere
  • Chore Warsaw Western Station is nearby - trains
  • Good transport accessibility by trams

Cons

  • No metro is the biggest minus
  • The old housing stock dominates
  • Less developed trade and restaurant infrastructure
  • Allergy to noise: Aleje Jerozolimskie is noisy

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent at Ochota Warsaw?

Ochota 2026: PLN 80-110/m²/month. Studio: PLN 2,700-3,800, two-room: PLN 4,000-5,800. 15–20% lower than Mokotów with a similar central location.

How to get from Ochota to the center without the metro?

Trams 7 and 9 take 10-15 minutes to Centrum. Bus 128 — to Mokotów in 10 minutes. Aleje Jerozolimskie is a 5-10 minute walk.

Ochota or Mokotów — which is more profitable?

Ochota is 15–20% cheaper at a similar distance from the center. Mokotów - metro M1, more greenery and a higher standard of the new fund. For a more budget option in the center - Ochota.
