About the district

Vesola Warsaw for rent is the smallest district of Warsaw with mostly private manor buildings and a real "rural" character. Wesoła joined Warsaw in 2002, but never acquired city features. There are more houses than apartments, more gardens than sidewalks.

There are practically no apartments for long-term rent - most of the ads are for rooms in houses or small cottages. This fundamentally complicates the search for those who are used to the apartment market.

Prices are the absolute minimum in Warsaw: a two-room apartment for 2,800–3,500 PLN. But this is rather a rarity than a rule. KM to the center — 35–45 minutes.

Transport and location

Wesoła is the easternmost part of Warsaw, bordering Rembertów (west) and Mińsk Mazowiecki outside the city. It is 22-25 km to the centrum.

  • KM Wesoła, Stara Miłosna — to Centrum 35–45 min
  • Buses: 121 — irregular
  • By car: S17/E67 — to the center 25–30 min
  • Bicycle on forest paths

Who is Wesoła for?

  • Those who are ready to sacrifice everything for the minimum price
  • Pensioners and people looking for absolute peace
  • Car owners without daily trips
  • Nature and forest lovers

Pros

  • The lowest rental price in Warsaw
  • Nature, silence, rural character
  • KM to the center — 35–45 minutes
  • Very few people - maximum privacy

Cons

  • There are practically no apartments for rent
  • It is impossible to live comfortably without a car
  • The smallest infrastructure of all 18 districts
  • Cut off from the city rhythm

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent at Wesoła 2026?

Wesoła 2026: 56–75 PLN/m²/month. Two-room apartment (if you can find it): PLN 2,800-3,500. But the offer of apartments here is minimal.

Are there apartments for rent on Wesoła?

Very little. Most of the real estate is private houses and estates. Otodom shows single ads per month. If you need an apartment, consider Rembertów or Wawer.

Is Wesoła suitable for families?

Only if you have a car and want to live in a semi-rural atmosphere. There is a school and a kindergarten, but the choice is minimal.
