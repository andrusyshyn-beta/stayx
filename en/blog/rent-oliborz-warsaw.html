About the district

Zholibozh Warsaw for rent is the most prestigious and the smallest of the central districts of the capital. Żoliborz is known for its coziness, interwar constructivist architecture and reputation as a 'smart' Warsaw address. Famous writers, cultural figures and journalists lived here - this atmosphere has been preserved.

The rental supply here is chronically limited: a good 2-bedroom apartment disappears in 7-10 days. Prices are second only to Śródmieścia: PLN 95-130/m²/month. YoY price growth has slowed (-1.7% per Tabelaofert.pl April 2026), but demand remains higher than supply.

A typical tenant: a top manager or senior professional, a family with children who value peace and security without being separated from the center.

Transport and location

Żoliborz is north of Śródmieście, between Wisłą (east) and Bemowo (west). Dworzec Centralny is 20 minutes away on the M1. Bordering Bielany to the north is a quieter green transition zone.

  • M1: 5 stations (Dworzec Gdański → Stare Bielany)
  • Trams: Mickiewicza, Jana Kazimierza
  • Buses: W-Z, Cytadela, Wisłostrada
  • Cycle paths along the Wisły — Bulwary Wiślane

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does it cost to rent in Zholibozh in 2026?

Rent in Żoliborz — 95–130 PLN/m²/month. Studio: PLN 3,500-5,000, one-room: PLN 4,200-6,000, two-room: PLN 5,500-8,000. The district is the second most expensive after Śródmieście. Source: Investropa, Tabelaofert.pl, Q1 2026.

How to quickly find an apartment in Zholibozh?

Good apartments on Żoliborz disappear in 7-10 days. Recommended: ready PDF portfolio, proof of income and willingness to sign within 24 hours of review.

Is Żoliborz suitable for families with children?

Yes, it is one of the best options for families: rated schools, safe streets, Plac Wilsona for walking, Cytadela. But the budget should be from PLN 6,000/month for 2-3 rooms.

What is the difference between Żoliborzem and Mokotów?

Żoliborz is smaller, more intimate, slightly more expensive per m², but with a smaller selection. Mokotów is bigger and more diverse, with better M1 coverage and more to offer. Both are great for families.
