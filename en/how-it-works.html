Your Ideal Home
in 8 Simple Steps

We have turned the complex process of renting housing in Warsaw into a transparent and safe online journey.

✉️
Step 01

Application

Leave a request for the "Online Rental" service through the form on the website or in Telegram. Our manager will contact you within 15 minutes.

📋
Step 02

Questionnaire

Answer a few basic questions (budget, area, number of rooms). This allows us to accurately match your needs to the market.

🔍
Step 03

Apartment Search

We begin searching for the most suitable options for you, using our internal databases and wide partner networks.

📹
Step 04

Online Viewing

We arrange with the owner and visit the address. In online mode, we show you everything: from plumbing condition to parking and area views.

⚖️
Step 05

Legal Audit

STAYX agent thoroughly checks the lease agreement for any "pitfalls," protecting your interests and ensuring compliance with Polish law.

✍️
Step 06

Remote Contract

Sign the agreement quickly and securely using the DocuSign service. This is a modern way to formalize arrangements from anywhere in the world.

💳
Step 07

Reservation Payment

After signing the contract, you pay a reservation fee (20%). This guarantees the owner will hold the apartment specially for you.

🔑
Step 08

Key Handover

On move-in day, our agent meets you at the property. You sign the handover protocol, check the condition, and receive the keys to your new home.
